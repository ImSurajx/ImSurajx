## 🧠 Early Engineering Experience (Custom ROM Development)

I was actively involved in an Android Custom ROM community where I:

- Worked on **unofficial ROM builds** for real users
- Learned advanced **Git & GitHub workflows** (cherry-pick, rebasing, conflict resolution)
- Understood complex codebases involving **device trees, kernels, vendor blobs, and build systems**
- Maintained an **unofficial Pixel-based ROM** for 6–8 months
- Collaborated with developers globally
- Raised the **highest community donation at that time** to repair my development device, with support from contributors outside India

This experience taught me **problem-solving under pressure, persistence, collaboration, and self-learning** — skills I now apply to software development.
